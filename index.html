Why accounts slide — the controllable drivers

Service strain, on large accounts. Slipping Green accounts carry roughly 4× the support cases (45 vs 10), 3× the service requests (17 vs 4.5) and more escalations — and they’re big ($607k vs $156k ARR). CSAT is already lower before the move. The slide is mid‑term, not renewal‑driven (renewal ~590 days out). You can see these coming.

Why accounts climb — and why you can’t bank on it

Relationship, not metrics. Recoverers trend toward higher CSAT (8.3 vs 7.1), cleaner balances (no past‑due), lighter ticket loads and smaller size — but none reaches significance. Recovery looks driven by CS intervention quality and stated‑reason resolution (Service Quality, Value, Pricing) that the dataset doesn’t quantify. Recovery is earned, not predicted.
