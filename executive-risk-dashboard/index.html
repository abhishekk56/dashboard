Deterioration Drivers

Genuine leading indicators of elevated risk, separated from mechanical artefacts. Bars = strength of association (effect size / model odds).
Why: the pattern is operational, not commercial. Risk is manufactured by service failure — SLA misses, escalations and ticket load — amplified by Mobile-platform complexity and account size. Customers don't leave because of price; they leave because delivery hurts.

Recovery Drivers

What separates accounts that climbed back to Green from those that stayed elevated.
Why: recovery is relationship-led. The cleanest differentiator is Business Reviews completed (recovered accounts had ≥1 QBR in 12 months; stuck accounts had none). Falling escalation volume and restored sentiment follow. Cadence and presence drive the turnaround.

Segment Risk Analysis

Elevated-risk rate (bars) against ARR exposure (line) by segment, platform and account value.
Read: risk rises monotonically with value — Key Customers run 23% elevated and accounts >$200K run 18%, while sub-$25K accounts are ~1%. Mobile is the structural hotspot: 16% elevated and $79M ARR, versus 2% for Fixed. The exposure equation is unambiguous: big + Mobile = the risk frontier, and that is exactly where retention spend earns its highest return.
